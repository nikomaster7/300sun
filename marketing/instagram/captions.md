## 01-300-days
Valencia gets about 300 days of sun a year. I live here, and I take visitors on private walks through the old town. Just your group, at your pace. Link in bio.

## 02-back-on-time
Coming to Valencia on a cruise? The walk is planned backwards from your all-aboard time. If we're ever running tight, I put you in a taxi to the terminal and pay the fare. Link in bio to pick your port day.

## 03-look-up
San Nicolás, in Valencia's old town. Private walks with a local: we start where you like, stop when you're hungry and skip whatever doesn't interest you.

## 04-one-price
One price for the group, not per head. €150 for up to 4 people for 3 hours, €200 for 4 hours, €300 for 6. A €30 deposit holds the day and the rest is paid at the end of the walk.

## 05-old-town-facts
Three things to look for in Valencia's old town: the 207 steps of the Micalet, the Water Tribunal on Thursdays at noon, and the gargoyles of La Lonja. I'll show you where to look.

## 06-eat-something-real
You see the good parts and you eat something real: the Mercado Central, horchata with fartons, and seafood paella afterwards if you're hungry.

## 07-your-route
Got four or six hours? Message me, tell me what you like, and we build the route together. Valencia is flat, so it works for kids and grandparents too.

## 08-groups
Bringing a group? Three partner hostels, more than 200 beds, with walks and paella arranged in the same chat. Schools, study abroad and church trips welcome. Send dates and numbers.
