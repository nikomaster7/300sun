# Selling 300sun in the USA: campaign plan

**Goal:** Americans who will be in Valencia, above all cruise passengers, book the walk (and the paella) **directly on 300sun.com**, not through a platform.

**Why it works:** Mediterranean cruise passengers book their shore excursions **3 to 9 months ahead**, and they plan in groups (Cruise Critic, Facebook). They also spend: your last clients paid about €190 + €80 in tips without blinking.

---

## 1. Before you travel (the basics, 1–2 weeks)
- [ ] Domain **300sun.com**, email, site live on Vercel (see domain-and-email.md)
- [ ] Cal.com connected, so people can book with no back-and-forth (see booking-setup.md)
- [ ] **Google Business Profile** "300sun – Private Walks & Paella Valencia" (free). Reviews matter here more than anywhere else.
- [ ] **TripAdvisor** listing (free). Americans check it before booking.
- [ ] Ask your recent clients (City Unscripted, paella) for a **Google review**. Without breaking the platform's rules: only people who already have your WhatsApp.
- [ ] **Business cards with a QR code** pointing to `300sun.com/?utm_source=card` (so we know where each visit came from)
- [ ] Show prices in dollars as well ("€150 · about $165"). I can add that to the site.

## 2. In the USA, in person (this is your advantage)
Nobody at City Unscripted is going to knock on doors. You can.
1. **Travel agencies and travel advisors** (independent ones, especially cruise specialists). They need trustworthy local guides for their clients (FIT, meaning independent travellers they plan trips for). Offer them **10% commission** on every client they send. That's still cheaper for you than any platform.
2. **Universities and study-abroad offices.** Groups that go to Spain need **accommodation** (your 200+ beds), **paella** and **walks**: your three products in one sale. Take a one-page PDF (I can make it from the site).
3. **High schools with Spanish-class trips, churches, clubs, retiree groups.** The same pitch for groups.
4. **Cruise conventions / meetups** if any fall on your dates, and travel shows.

Take: cards with a QR code, a tablet or phone with the site in **presentation mode (press P)**, and a paella photo that makes them hungry.

## 3. Online, aimed at the USA
| Channel | What to do | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| **Cruise Critic roll calls** | Join the forums for ships stopping in Valencia in 2027. Answer questions about Valencia and be useful, not salesy. Satisfied clients recommend you there. | €0 |
| **Facebook groups** for cruises and ships (MSC, Royal Caribbean, Celebrity, Norwegian, Princess) | Same approach: help first, and share the link when someone asks | €0 |
| **Google Ads** | Searches like "Valencia shore excursion private", "Valencia private tour from cruise port", "paella class Valencia". Few searches, but people with a card in hand. | €5–10/day to start |
| **Instagram / TikTok** | Short videos: the paella being cooked, the socarrat, the Water Tribunal, the Micalet from the top. In English. | €0 + time |
| **Meta Ads** (Facebook/Instagram) | Audience: USA, ages 45–70, interested in Mediterranean cruises. Best used for **retargeting** people who already visited the site. | €5/day, later |

**Important:** Meta Ads and Google Ads retargeting need a tracking pixel on the site. That means adding a **cookie banner** and changing the footer ("no cookies"). Start with Google Ads (no pixel needed to begin) and add the pixel only when you're ready to spend on Meta.

## 4. One idea that's very hard for others to copy: a page per ship
Build pages like **"MSC World Europa in Valencia – 14 May 2027 – private walk back before all-aboard"**, one per ship call, using the port schedule (CruiseMapper). Whoever searches for their ship + Valencia finds you. I can generate these automatically from the schedule.

## 5. Measuring
- Every link with `?utm_source=` (card, facebook, cruisecritic, google, agency-name)
- Count: WhatsApp messages, Cal.com bookings, and where each one came from (add a Cal.com question: "How did you hear about us?")
- A weekly review: what brought bookings gets more time or money; what didn't gets dropped.

## Suggested budget for the first 3 months
- Domain + email: ~€10 + ~€7/month
- Cards: ~€30
- Google Ads: ~€200–300 per month
- **Total: under €1,000**, which is roughly 4–6 walks sold directly.
