## What it is

A small travel agency in **Valencia** that sells experiences directly to travellers. No flights, no tickets. Later expansion: Madrid, then Barcelona.

The website does two jobs:
1. **Authority**: presentation material for groups, schools and cruise passengers (it can be shown full-screen like a deck).
2. **Direct sales**: every service has a clear "Request / Book" action (WhatsApp, email, form).

## Services and how each one earns money

| # | Service | Target client | How we earn |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Group accommodation**: 200+ beds across 3 hostels | Student and college groups, school trips, groups coming for paella, culture and beach | Commission from the hostel owner on groups we bring in (see below) |
| 2 | **Paella workshops**: an authentic Valencian paella experience | Groups, couples, cruise passengers | Commission per guest |
| 3 | **Private personalised walks / tours** | US and UK travellers, cruise passengers, families | Direct sale: we keep 100% instead of the ~30–40% the platforms pay |
| 4 | **Online language courses**: English, Spanish, Italian | Travellers, students, expats | Own course _Back to Basics_ (Nicolás Maldonado, $19.99, [Cursum](https://www.cursum.ec/curso-dictado/ingles-back-to-basics)) plus referral commission on partner teachers' courses |

## Accommodation commission proposal (draft, to discuss)

- Booking.com takes roughly **15–20%** from the hostel on every booking.
- The current group department (lead handling) earns **2%**.
- **Proposal:** **10% commission on new groups that we source ourselves**, paid on stay revenue.
  - The hostel still saves 5–10 points versus Booking.com, with no guest-acquisition cost.
  - Existing inbound leads stay at the current 2%, or a small raise for handling.
  - Optional tiers: 10% by default, 12% for groups of 50+ beds, 15% for repeat institutional clients (schools, universities) we bring back each year.
- Get it **in writing before** the first group arrives, attached to that first client as proof.
