# Priority 1: guide licence (habilitación de guía de turismo)

## What we know (checked 2026-10-09, Generalitat FAQ of February 2026)
- **No exam is open.** The last call was May 2024 and none is active.
- **There is now a second way, without an exam: "habilitación directa"** (Ley 6/2024, art. 126). You need both:
  1. **Language:** a certificate of at least **B2 in a foreign language**. People who are not Spanish nationals also need B2 in Spanish or Valencian.
  2. **One of these qualifications:** Grado or Diplomatura in Turismo; Técnico Superior en Guía, Información y Asistencias Turísticas; degrees in History, Art History, Geography, Humanities, Fine Arts, Archaeology or Cultural Management; Técnico Superior en Agencias de Viajes y Gestión de Eventos; or an equivalent.
- **With any other degree** (including a foreign one with its homologación), you qualify by adding one accredited unit: **UC2579_3**, from the certificate *Guía de Turistas y Visitantes (HOT335_3)*. It can be accredited through work experience or by studying that single unit in vocational training (FP).
- **What the licence is required for:** visiting and explaining places declared *Bien de Interés Cultural* (BIC). Accompanying, informing and assisting a group on a route does not require it. That is why the site says "private walk with a local" and you wait at the door of the monuments.
- The card is renewed every five years. Contact: guiadeturisme@gva.es.
