# Priority 3: sales strategy for the USA trip (28 Oct – 13 Nov 2026)

Background: `docs/usa-campaign.md`, `docs/agencias/como-funciona.md`, and the competitor check of 2026-10-09 (private 3–4 h tours in Valencia sell for $346–$686 on ToursByLocals; cruise lines charge from $1,399 per vehicle).

## The idea in one line
Come back with travel advisors who have your link and have met you, because cruise passengers book 3–9 months ahead and advisors sell to them all year.
