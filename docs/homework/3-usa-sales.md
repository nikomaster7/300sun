# Priority 3: sales strategy for the USA trip (28 Oct – 13 Nov 2026)

Background: `docs/usa-campaign.md`, `docs/agencias/como-funciona.md`, and the competitor check of 2026-10-09 (private 3–4 h tours in Valencia sell for $346–$686 on ToursByLocals; cruise lines charge from $1,399 per vehicle).

## The idea in one line
Come back with travel advisors who have your link and have met you, because cruise passengers book 3–9 months ahead and advisors sell to them all year.

## What is ready
- The 40-second pitch and the rate table per group and length.
- The advisors page (300sun.com/advisors) and the one-pager PDF with the 15% commission.
- Per-agency links: `300sun.com/cruise/?ref=agency-name`.
- The route sheet, to show as "what your client receives".

## To decide with Claude before the trip
- [ ] **You: where exactly are you going** (cities, dates) and how many free days you have for visits. The plan depends on it.
- [ ] **Claude: build the target list** for those cities: independent travel agencies and advisors who sell Mediterranean cruises, with address, contact and a one-line reason. Goal: 15–20 to contact, 5–8 visits.
- [ ] **Claude: write the emails** to send before you fly ("I'm in town on these dates, 15 minutes?") and the follow-up for after each visit.
- [ ] Decide the **premium package** for advisors' clients: private car from the terminal and back, the walk, horchata and a tasting included, one all-in price. Advisors sell a package more easily than a €150 walk.
- [ ] Confirm the wording of the **back-on-board promise** (taxi paid by you) and the **advisor terms** (15%, paid within 7 days, by PayPal or bank transfer).
- [ ] Decide how each visit is recorded. Claude can add an "Agencies visited" tab to the commission sheet (name, contact, date, their link, next step).

## To prepare and pack
- [ ] You: print 30 one-pagers (download again first: it now has the rate table) and 10 example route sheets.
- [ ] You: set up the commission sheet in Google Sheets (`docs/agencias/google-sheets-setup.md`) and test `300sun.com/cruise/?ref=test-agency` on your phone.
- [ ] You: get 3–5 Google reviews before leaving. Advisors will look you up.
- [ ] Claude: a small card with a QR code that opens 300sun.com/advisors, if you want something to leave behind.

## While you're away
- [ ] Google profile shows you as closed for those dates (Cal.com already does).
- [ ] After each visit, message Claude the agency's name. Claude sends back their link and the follow-up email.

## Also from the marketing list
