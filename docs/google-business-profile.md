Go to **business.google.com/create** and fill in:

Your home address may be asked for verification only. **It is never shown publicly** if you answered "No" above.

## 2. Verify
Google chooses the method. For a business with no office it's usually a **video**:
- One continuous recording, no cuts, 1–2 minutes.
- Show: the street sign or a landmark where you work (Torres de Serranos is perfect, as that's the meeting point), something branded (300sun.com open on your phone, the logo, the Cal.com booking page), and that you manage the business (open the Gmail inbox or Cal.com dashboard).
- Review usually takes 1–5 working days. Edits are locked while it's being reviewed, so get everything below in first if Google lets you.

Don't add "Tour guide" until the licence arrives.
