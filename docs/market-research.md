# Market research: private tours and direct sales in Valencia

_Last updated: 2026-09-23_

## 1. The opportunity in one paragraph

Platforms like City Unscripted, Guydeez, Lokafy and ToursByLocals find the traveller, charge a premium, and pay the local guide only part of it. They also resell the same tours on Viator and GetYourGuide, which keep another **20–30%** in commission. The guide does all the work and gets the smallest share. Selling directly (own website, Google, TripAdvisor, cruise forums) removes both layers.

**Real example (22 Sep 2026):** a 6-hour private walk through City Unscripted for 2 Americans. They added 2 friends on the spot.
- Platform payout: €102
- Direct add-on for 2 extra guests: €90
- Tips: €80
- **Total: €272 for 6 hours.** The direct add-on (€90 for 2 people) came close to the platform's payout for the whole tour. That gap is the business.

## 2. The platforms (intermediaries)

| Platform | Model | What they charge / pay | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **City Unscripted** | Private, personalised tours with a local "host". Single price for the guest, no extra fees shown. | Sets a minimum price per city. The guide's fee comes out of a "base price" agreed with a Creator Manager. Extras (transport, tickets, food) are not part of the guide's fee. | Mostly US and UK customers, and many cruise passengers. The guide has no control over the final price. |
| **Guydeez** (Guydeez Travel SL, Spain) | Private tours with a local guide in 80+ destinations; can also book monument tickets. | Commission not published. | Also sells on **GetYourGuide** as a supplier, so the traveller pays the GYG markup on top. Spanish company, strong in Barcelona. |
| **Lokafy** | "Private walking tours with locals" in 300+ cities. The guide is a resident, not a professional guide. | Fixed price tiers by duration. Children under 3 free, 3–12 half price. Also sells through Viator. | Also sells to travel advisors (FIT clients): a B2B channel we can copy. |
| **ToursByLocals** | Marketplace of licensed private guides. | Valencia examples: 6-hour private excursion **$650–791 for up to 6 people**. | Clearest public benchmark for what a private day in Valencia is worth. |

### The OTAs (Online Travel Agencies) behind them

| OTA | Supplier commission (2026) |
|---|---|
| Viator | 20% base, 25% common default; "Accelerate" promotion raises it to 30–35% |
| GetYourGuide | ~30% starting rate; high-volume suppliers negotiate down to 25–28% |

When a platform like Guydeez or Lokafy sells through Viator or GetYourGuide, **the traveller's money is split three ways**: OTA, then platform, then guide.

## 3. The cruise market in Valencia

- **2025:** 799,050 cruise passengers and 267 ship calls (+3.2% passengers vs 2024).
- Spain overall passed 14 million cruise passengers in 2025, a record.
- From 2026 the port runs **two cruise berths** (Transversal and Poniente docks), so capacity is growing.
- MSC uses Valencia as a **home port** (turnaround), with departures almost every week in Oct–Nov 2026. This means two segments:
  1. **Transit passengers** (ship in port for 6–10 hours) → private walking tours, paella experience. Main fear: _missing the ship_.
  2. **Turnaround passengers** (start or end the cruise here) → a pre- or post-cruise night in Valencia, day tour, paella. A possible accommodation cross-sell.

### How to reach cruise passengers directly

1. **Cruise Critic "Roll Calls"**: forums per ship and sailing date where passengers organise private tours together **months in advance**. This is where American groups of 4–12 people form. Be present with a good reputation and follow each forum's rules (vendors usually can't post adverts; passengers recommend you).
2. **Facebook groups** per ship and per cruise line ("MSC Cruise Fans", "Royal Caribbean Mediterranean 2027", etc.).
3. **Google Business Profile + TripAdvisor**: cruise passengers search "Valencia private tour from cruise port" and read reviews.
4. **Our own website** with a clear promise: _"Back to the ship on time, guaranteed"_, pickup at the terminal, and price per group (not per person).
5. **Repeat and referral**: every happy City Unscripted client already shows direct demand (see the example above). Hand out a card or QR code and collect a WhatsApp contact for friends and family.
6. **B2B**: US travel advisors (like Lokafy's FIT programme) who book private guides for their clients.

Cruise schedules to plan around: [CruiseMapper Valencia](https://www.cruisemapper.com/ports/valencia-port-678), [CruiseTimetables 2026](https://www.cruisetimetables.com/valencia-spain-cruise-ship-schedule-2026.html).

## 4. Legal point to solve early: the guide licence

In the Comunitat Valenciana, a **professional tour guide needs the official habilitación**. You can get it by passing the Generalitat's exam (100-question test, then an oral case study) or by validating official training. You need **B2 in a foreign language** and B2 in Spanish or Valencian. The last exam call was in **May 2024**, and no call is open right now. Contact: guiadeturisme@gva.es.

Until you have the licence:
- Present the tours as **"local host experiences / city walks with a local"**, the same way Lokafy and City Unscripted do, not as "official guided tours".
- Don't guide **inside** monuments where only licensed guides may explain.
- Once you are licensed, the wording "official licensed guide" becomes a strong selling point, and hiring other guides becomes straightforward.

## 5. Recommended direct pricing (starting point)

| Service | Suggested direct price | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Private walk, 3 h, 1–4 people | €180 per group | Below the Viator per-person price for couples and families, and roughly double what the platforms pay you |
| Private walk, 6 h / cruise day, 1–4 people | €340 per group | Well below ToursByLocals ($650+), still about 3× the platform payout |
| Extra person (5–8) | +€30 per person | Matches what clients already accepted (€45 per person) |
| Paella experience | €65–75 per person | Market rate. Commission agreed with the restaurant or chef |
| Walk + paella combo | €340 for 2 people | The highest-margin package and the easiest to sell to cruise passengers |

Tips come on top. Accept card, Bizum, PayPal and Stripe. Ask for a 30% deposit to confirm.
