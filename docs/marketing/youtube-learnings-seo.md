# What 5 YouTubers teach us about growing 300sun (SEO, AI search, vibe coding)

_Researched 6 Oct 2026._

**How this was made:** YouTube is blocked from Claude's working environment, so the videos were **not watched**. Each summary comes from search-indexed descriptions, transcript notes and summaries, and the creators' companion articles and reports. Where the content couldn't be confirmed, it says so. The videos are worth watching yourself: the links are below.

**Choice of creators:** quality over fame, and up to date with AI search and AI coding in 2025–26. Jeff Su and Chris Raroque as asked, plus:
- Ahrefs, for SEO and AI search, with original data.
- Darren Shaw (Whitespark), for local SEO. He's the most relevant to a walk business in one city.
- Greg Isenberg, for distribution and "vibe marketing".

## 1. Ahrefs: SEO for the AI-search era
The most data-driven SEO channel. Their 2025–26 videos are about getting cited by Google AI Overviews, ChatGPT and Perplexity ("AEO", answer engine optimization).

**a) [Complete AI SEO Course for Beginners: Learn AEO in 86 Minutes](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uza9GX0E2mw)** (also as the [AEO course](https://ahrefs.com/academy/aeo-course))
- Covers how AI search works, prompt research, content, brand mentions, technical AEO and measurement. Built on a 75,000-brand study and 55.8M AI Overviews.
- AI engines recommend brands that are **mentioned** a lot across the web. Mentions matter as much as links.
- **For 300sun:** get "300sun" named, with the same one-line description, in many places: Google Business Profile, TripAdvisor, Cruise Critic threads, blogs, YouTube. The more consistent the description, the more an AI repeats it.

**b) [YouTube SEO for AI Search: How to Rank your YouTube Videos](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_b_cTpLHMTM)**
- YouTube is the most cited domain in Google AI Overviews: up to ~29.5% of them, per BrightEdge data quoted alongside it.
- In Ahrefs' brand study, mentions in YouTube titles, descriptions and **transcripts** correlated most with AI Overview visibility.
- AI engines read the **transcript**, not the picture.
- **For 300sun:** short vertical videos where you **say** the words people search ("Valencia shore excursion", "from the cruise port to the old town", "back on board on time"). Always upload subtitles.

**c) [Keyword & Prompt Research for AI SEO (AEO)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=b8PyeJOE--Y)**
- Research the **questions** people type into ChatGPT, not just 2-word keywords. Answer each one clearly on a page.
- (The detailed content of this one couldn't be confirmed beyond its description.)
- **For 300sun:** one page or FAQ entry per real cruise question. See the keyword list at the end.

## 2. Darren Shaw (Whitespark): local SEO
Darren runs the yearly **Local Search Ranking Factors** survey (47 experts, 187 factors). The 2026 edition added an "AI search visibility" category. He explains it on several podcasts.

**a) [Darren Shaw Breaks Down the 2026 Ranking Factors: AI Search, Reviews, and the Future of Local SEO](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dWeyZIzk5ts)**
- **Business hours are the #5 factor** for the Google Maps "local pack". Google prefers businesses that are **open at the moment of the search**.
- New in 2026: reviews **with videos**, and videos relevant to your categories. AI-generated photos on a profile are now tracked as a negative.
- **For 300sun:** set real opening hours on the Google Business Profile, for example 8:00–20:00 every day, since cruise days include weekends. Add real short videos, and ask happy guests for a review with a short clip.

**b) [Breaking Down the 2026 Local Search Ranking Factors with Darren Shaw](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SGP0E2ciPNM)** (Local Marketing Secrets)
- Reviews became a **recency and frequency** game. A steady stream of new reviews beats a big pile of old ones.
- The top **AI visibility** factors:
  - being on expert-curated **"best of" lists**
  - a **dedicated page for each service**
  - mentions on industry-relevant sites
  - reviews on authoritative third-party sites
- Three of the top five are citations: "mentions are the new links".
- **For 300sun:**
  - Ask for a review after **every** walk, not in batches.
  - Keep separate pages: /cruise (the walk), a future paella page, groups.
  - Get onto "best private tours in Valencia" lists by writing to the bloggers who publish them.

**c) [Local SEO Masterclass: Darren Shaw on Ranking Factors, Reviews, and Google Business Hacks](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1awWMG1e5kY)** (The Edward Show)
- Keep the profile complete and fresh: photos, regular **Posts** (offers, reviews, news).
- Answer every review, and negative ones calmly and quickly.
- Engagement counts: people looking at photos, watching videos, and not bouncing back to the results.
- **For 300sun:** one Google Business Profile post a week (a photo from that day's walk plus one line). It's 5 minutes, and it keeps the profile "alive".

## 3. Greg Isenberg: distribution and "vibe marketing"
Former Reddit and TikTok advisor and host of the Startup Ideas Podcast. He's good on finding customers without ads.

**a) [I'm REVEALING ALL the Vibe Marketing Secrets (No Gatekeeping)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=S8a7gkFhoBA)**
- "Vibe marketing" means 1–4 people with AI tools doing what a marketing team used to do: AI writes, designs, cuts video and runs automations, and the human chooses the taste.
- Demos include Claude, Gumloop and no-code automation.
- **For 300sun:** one person can run the channels if it's systematic. Film once per walk, then let AI turn it into a Short, an Instagram post, a Google post and a Cruise Critic tip.

**b) Reddit + AI to find what people want** ([his 4-step thread with the 21-minute video](https://x.com/gregisenberg/status/1817634981939982461))
- Find active communities (10k–100k members are less crowded) and read what people repeatedly ask.
- Then look at the top YouTube videos in that niche (he suggests 20–25) to see what lands.
- **For 300sun:**
  - Read r/Cruise, r/valencia and r/travel, the Cruise Critic Valencia board, and the roll calls of 2027 ships.
  - Note every repeated question ("Is the shuttle worth it?", "Can I see the old town in 6 hours?").
  - Each one becomes an FAQ answer or a video.

**c) [Complete Guide to AI Marketing: Vibe Marketing 101](https://audioscrape.com/podcast/the-startup-ideas-podcast/episode/complete-guide-to-ai-marketing-vibe-marketing-101)** (podcast episode)
- The three pillars: AI tools for the heavy lifting (copy, targeting, testing), no-code automations to connect them, and modular tools that let a tiny team act like an agency.
- **For 300sun:** you already have the pieces. Cal.com, WhatsApp and the Google Sheet with the webhook are automations. Next could be an automatic review request the day after each walk.

## 4. Jeff Su: using AI well (ex-Google, productivity)
Clear, practical and always current. Useful for **you** working faster with ChatGPT, Gemini and NotebookLM.

**a) [Google's NEW Prompting Guide is Incredible!](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=o64Mv-ArFDI)**
- Google's framework is **TCREI**: Task, Context, References (examples), Evaluate, Iterate.
- Good prompts average about **21 words**. Most people write under 9.
- Ask for **3 variations**, break big jobs into small steps, and start broad, then narrow down.
- **For 300sun:** for each social post or reply to an agency, give the AI the task, who it's for, an example you liked, and ask for 3 versions.

**b) [Learn 80% of NotebookLM in Under 13 Minutes!](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EOmgC3-hznM)**
- NotebookLM shines when you can't afford made-up answers and your information is scattered (PDFs, web pages, YouTube links).
- It only answers from the sources you give it. Good sources give good answers.
- **For 300sun:** make a "Valencia cruise" notebook with the port schedule, Cruise Critic threads, your reviews and this file. Ask it for FAQs and video scripts that stick to facts.

**c) [Master the Perfect ChatGPT Prompt Formula (in just 8 minutes)!](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jC4v5AS4RIM)** (older, still his most-used method)
- Six parts, in order of importance: **task, context, exemplar, persona, format, tone**.
- **For 300sun:** write the persona once ("a local in Valencia who runs private walks, writes short first-person lines, never says 'guided tour'") and reuse it in every prompt.

Also new from him: [The CORRECT way to use ChatGPT (in 2026)](https://www.youtube.com/live/p3840QxlYzc) and [I Built a NotebookLM + Gemini Workflow That Makes Prompt Engineering Pointless](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=W-rtNL_Uf3I). Their content couldn't be confirmed from text sources. By the titles, the theme is "give the AI context documents instead of clever prompts".

## 5. Chris Raroque: building with AI (Claude Code + Cursor)
A solo developer of productivity apps who shows his real AI coding workflow. He's honest about what fails.

**a) [How I Get AI To Follow My Designs (In-Depth Walkthrough)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=18V3lFePdWU)**
- He gave the same design to 3 AI coding tools with the same prompt and compared the results.
- The lesson: AI follows a design when the **design rules are written down** (colors, spacing, components) in a file the AI reads, for example a DESIGN.md.
- **For 300sun:** the site's rules live in `CLAUDE.md` and `docs/site-decisions.md`. A short `DESIGN.md` with the colors, fonts, rays and photo rules would keep every future AI change on-brand.

**b) [How I Design Apps 10x Better](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jSWuepkuFrU)** (his "design secrets")
- Small details make a product feel better: subtle **animation**, custom illustrations for empty states, consistent icons, haptics, and looking at good design every day.
- **For 300sun:** the site already has the rising fade-in and the sun-3 rays. One more detail could be the rays "lighting up" when you open an FAQ. Keep it subtle.

**c) [How I Use Claude Code & Cursor to Ship 10x Faster](https://podcasts.apple.com/ie/podcast/how-i-use-claude-code-cursor-ship-10x-faster/id1593424985?i=1000739173517)** (on Greg Isenberg's podcast)
- He uses both tools side by side because they're good at different things.
- **For 300sun:** what we already do. Small changes, checked on a phone and a desktop, then published.

Bonus: he published [vibe-security-skill](https://github.com/raroque/vibe-security-skill), an AI skill that audits vibe-coded apps for common security mistakes. 300sun is a static site with a strict security policy, so the risk is low. It's still worth running once before adding anything that stores data.

## Search words to target
These are **candidates**, not measured. Volumes weren't available from here. Check them for free in Google Keyword Planner or Ahrefs' free keyword generator before building pages.

**Cruise (highest intent):** valencia shore excursions · private shore excursion valencia · valencia cruise port to city center · valencia cruise port taxi · things to do in valencia on a cruise · valencia in one day from cruise ship · can you walk from valencia cruise port · valencia cruise port shuttle · [ship name] valencia port day

**The walk:** private tour valencia old town · valencia old town walk · valencia with a local · valencia cathedral holy grail · valencia central market tour · horchata valencia where to try

**Food:** paella cooking class valencia · authentic paella valencia · where to eat paella valencia

**Mediterranean context:** best mediterranean cruise ports spain · valencia vs barcelona cruise port · western mediterranean cruise valencia

Wording rule: the site says "private walk with a local", not "guided tour", until the licence arrives. Pages can still answer a search like "private tour valencia", as long as the text describes what you actually do.

**Context numbers:** Valencia had about 21 ship calls and 68,000 passengers and crew in August 2026, 18 calls in September and 9 in December ([Gangwaze](https://gangwaze.com/cruise-port-schedule/valencia-spain)). Ships stay 6–8 hours.
