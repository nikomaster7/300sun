# "Skip the €50 Paella": cruise passenger campaign

**Status:** draft, to work on later
**Goal:** cruise passengers docking in Valencia book a 300sun city walk **directly on 300sun.com** and finish it with authentic paella at a partner restaurant in the centre for about **€20 per person**, instead of paying €50+ at a tourist spot near the port.

**Why it works:** cruise passengers have 6–9 hours in Valencia and worry about two things: wasting money in tourist traps and missing the ship. This offer solves both.

---

## 1. Headlines

**Main:**
> **Don't pay €50 for paella. Pay €20 for the real one.**
> Book our city walk. We'll take you to the best paella in the centre of Valencia.

**Alternatives to test:**
- "Paella was born in Valencia. Don't let it rob you."
- "Your ship docks at 8. Real paella at 1:30. Back on board by 5."
- "Tourist-trap paella: €50+. Paella the locals eat: €20. You choose."
- "Walk like a local. Eat like a Valencian."
- "The best paella in Valencia isn't on the seafront."

## 2. Ad copy (social / flyer)

> ⚓ **Docking in Valencia?**
> Most cruise visitors pay €50+ for paella by the port. You don't have to.
>
> Join our **[X]-hour city walk** through the old town: the Cathedral, the Central Market, the Silk Exchange and Turia Gardens. Then sit down where locals eat for **authentic Valencian paella at €20 per person.**

> ✅ Private / small group ✅ English-speaking guide ✅ Planned around your ship's schedule
>
> 👉 Book at 300sun.com. Places are limited on cruise days.

**Story angle:** real *paella valenciana* is chicken, rabbit and garrofó beans, not seafood. Teach it during the walk. It makes us the local experts.

## 4. Offer: decide before launch
- [ ] Paella partner restaurant(s) in the centre: name, fixed group price, time slot, how fast they can serve
- [ ] Is the €20 paella **included in the walk price or paid on the spot**? Say it clearly. An all-inclusive "Walk + paella lunch: €XX" usually sells best
- [ ] Walk length and route that fit a cruise day (port ↔ centre is ~4–5 km, so account for the shuttle, taxi or tram time)
- [ ] Return time: "back at the port / shuttle stop by X". A formal **back-to-ship guarantee** waits until the guide licence arrives (see business-plan.md)
- [ ] Real paella photos (site/images/paella/)

## 5. Be careful with
- **Pricing claims.** Restaurants usually price paella **per person, often with a 2-person minimum**, around €18–30. Safer wording: "up to €50+ at tourist spots" or "€50+ for two on the seafront". Do not state it as a fact about every restaurant.
- **"Best paella in the city"** is fine as opinion. It's stronger with proof: "chosen by locals", "family-run since 19XX", or the restaurant's rating.
- **Never name competitors or restaurants negatively.**
- Guided-tour claims ("official guide") only after the licence.

## 6. Measuring
- A UTM on every link: `?utm_source=cruisecritic`, `facebook`, `google`, `flyer`, `instagram`
- Cal.com question: "How did you hear about us?"
- Test 2–3 headlines with a small budget and keep the winner.
