Questions

I'm on a cruise. Will I make it back?

I plan the walk around your all-aboard time and finish with plenty of margin. If we're ever running tight, I pay your taxi back to the port.

More than 4 people?

Up to 8. Each extra person is €10 an hour. Put the number in the booking.

How do I pay?

A €30 deposit by PayPal holds the day. The rest at the end of the walk, cash or PayPal. Cancel up to 48 hours before and I refund the deposit.

Is it OK for kids or grandparents?

Yes. Valencia is flat. We make it shorter and add coffee stops.

And if it rains?

The Mercado Central and the Lonja are covered, or we move to another day.
