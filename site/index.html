02 — Paella

Cook it. Fight over the socarrat.

Paella comes from the rice fields just south of Valencia, and people here have strong opinions about it. In the workshop you cook the original one from scratch, then sit down and eat it with a glass of wine.

The crispy layer stuck to the bottom of the pan is the socarrat. It's the best part, and there's never quite enough of it.

From €65 per person. Works for two people or for a school group of fifty.

Questions

Things people ask.

What if my ship is late or leaves early?

We plan the day around your all-aboard time, not the arrival time on the itinerary. You're back at the terminal 90 minutes before it. If the ship arrives late, we shorten the route. We don't cut into your buffer.

Is this an official guided tour?

It's a private walk with a local. We walk the streets together. For the inside of the Cathedral or the Lonja you go in with your own ticket (and the audio guide), and I'll be waiting at the door.

Is it OK for kids or grandparents?

Valencia is flat. Really flat. We can make the walk shorter, add more coffee stops and skip the 207 steps up the Micalet.

How do I pay?

A small deposit holds the date, and the rest is paid on the day. We sort out the details on WhatsApp.

And if it rains?

It doesn't rain much here. If it does, the Mercado Central and the Lonja are both covered, or we move you to another day if your plans allow it.

Can I do the paella without the walk?

Yes. Each thing can be booked on its own. Most people end up doing both, though.

We're a group of 120. Is that too many?

No. Send dates and numbers and we'll tell you what's free across the three hostels.
