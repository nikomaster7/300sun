How it works

You send them. I do the rest.

  1. Send your clients your own link, like 300sun.com/cruise/?ref=your-agency. Every booking made through it reaches me with your name on it. You can also book the day for them yourself and put your agency’s name in the notes.
  2. I confirm on WhatsApp with your clients: ship, all-aboard time, meeting point. They pay me a €30 deposit by PayPal and the rest at the end of the walk.
  3. Within 7 days of the walk, I pay you 15% of what your clients paid me, by PayPal or Wise. Tips, tickets and food on the day don’t count.

Questions

Good to know.

Is this an official guided tour?

It’s a private walk with a local. We walk the streets together. Inside the Cathedral or the Silk Exchange your clients use their own ticket and the audio guide, and I wait at the door.

What if the ship doesn’t dock, or they cancel?

If the ship doesn’t dock, or they cancel at least 48 hours before, I refund the deposit in full.

Older travellers, kids, wheelchairs?

Valencia is flat. We go slower, sit down more and skip the 207 steps up the bell tower. Tell me what your clients need and I’ll plan around it.

How do I get my own link?

Message me and I’ll send it. It’s 300sun.com/cruise/?ref= followed by your agency’s name in lowercase with dashes, for example ?ref=sunny-cruises. Your clients see the normal page; the booking just arrives with your name.

A bigger group?

Up to 8 people on one walk. For more, or for a group that needs beds, send me dates and numbers and I’ll put together a quote.
