Port day tips

Before you go ashore.

  1. Thursday: Silver Ray is in port 08:00–18:00. Your all-aboard will be earlier than 18:00: check it in your cruise line's app and work backwards.
  2. Friday is a turnaround day for MSC Sinfonia (embarkation and disembarkation). The terminal and taxi rank are busier than usual: allow extra time.
  3. Embarking on Friday evening? Drop your bags early and spend the day in the old town. The ship sails at 21:00.
  4. Sunday: the Central Market is closed. No ships in port this weekend.

Every week

Port day basics.

  1. You can’t walk out of the port. The cruise terminal is 4–6 km from the old town: take a taxi, your ship’s shuttle or a city bus. How to get in and back.
  2. Work backwards from all-aboard. Be back at the terminal an hour before it, and leave the old town about 30 minutes before that.
  3. The Central Market is closed on Sundays. On a Sunday port day, plan around it.
