Questions

Which paella do we cook?

The original paella valenciana: chicken, rabbit, flat green beans, garrofó beans, grated tomato, saffron and round rice from the Albufera. No chorizo, no peas, and no prawns with the chicken.

Do I need to know how to cook?

No. The cook talks you through every step, from the first oil in the pan to the socarrat.

How many people can come?

From two people to a group of 20.

How do I book?

Pick a date and how many you are, and press Book now. WhatsApp opens with the booking written. I check the day with the cook and confirm.

Can I add a walk?

Yes. Many people do the old town in the morning and the paella at lunch.
