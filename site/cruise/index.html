Questions

Before you book.

Will I make it back to the ship?

That's the one thing we plan first. Tell me your all-aboard time and the walk is timed backwards from it, with plenty of margin for the taxi back to the port. If the ship arrives late, we shorten the route. And if we're ever running tight, I put you in a taxi and pay the fare myself.

How do I get from the ship to the start?

Message me on WhatsApp and I book a taxi I trust to pick you up at the cruise terminal and bring you to Torres de Serranos. It's a short ride, and you pay the driver directly. I'll send you the driver's details and the exact meeting point. All the options, with times and prices, are in my cruise port guide.

How do I pay?

When you book, I send you a PayPal link for a €30 deposit. The rest you pay at the end of the walk, in cash or by PayPal. Museum tickets and food are separate, and only if you want them.

Is it OK for kids or older travellers?

Valencia is flat. We can go slower, sit down more and skip the 207 steps up the Micalet.

Is this an official guided tour?

It's a private walk with a local. We walk the streets together. Inside the Cathedral or the Lonja you use your own ticket and the audio guide, and I'll be waiting at the door.

What if it rains, or my port call is cancelled?

If the ship doesn't dock, I refund your deposit in full. Just message me. Rain is rare here. If it does rain, the market and the Lonja are covered.
